CG Shield

A privacy-aware approach to website traffic protection, combining local analysis, optional AI review and controlled mitigation. The focus is on transparent security decisions that remain under operator control.

Local-first analysisCloudflareOptional AI reviewPrivacy-aware design
OVERVIEW

Traffic protection with clear operational boundaries.

CG Shield brings request signals, protection state and review decisions into a single operator-focused workflow. Its design prioritises fast local checks before more expensive analysis.

ENGINEERING CHALLENGE

Balancing detection with privacy and proportionate action.

Suspicious requests require investigation, but permanent blocking or sending all traffic to external AI services would introduce avoidable risk, cost and complexity.

WORKFLOW

How it works

STEP 01

Analyse locally

Inspect traffic signals through lightweight rules before deciding whether additional review is warranted.

STEP 02

Review ambiguous activity

Reserve optional AI assessment for uncertain cases and retain the reasoning alongside the signal.

STEP 03

Apply bounded mitigation

Use observation, challenge and defence modes, with temporary actions and visible protection state.

ENGINEERING

Technical approach

Detection

Local-first rules analyse request signals without making AI a requirement for every decision.

AI integration

Optional second-stage review for cases where rule-based signals alone are inconclusive.

Edge controls

Cloudflare is the enforcement layer, with deliberately scoped protection and reversible interventions.

Observability

Operator-facing visibility into recent detections, decisions and the current protection mode.

WHY IT MATTERS

Security decisions should be useful, explainable and reversible.

The project brings together traffic analysis, privacy considerations and the practical limits of AI automation. It reflects an engineering approach that favours clear controls and operational oversight over unnecessary complexity.
