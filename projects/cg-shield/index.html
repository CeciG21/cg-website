CG Shield

A privacy-aware website traffic protection layer that starts with cheap local analysis and keeps automated mitigations bounded, explainable and reversible.

Local-first analysisCloudflareOptional AI reviewPrivacy controls
THE PROBLEM

Website protection often becomes expensive, opaque or too aggressive.

Small teams need a way to inspect suspicious traffic without sending every request to an AI model or permanently blocking visitors based on a single automated judgement.

THE APPROACH

Start local. Escalate only when useful.

CG Shield performs cheap local analysis first. Optional AI review is reserved for ambiguous cases, while Cloudflare provides the edge enforcement layer. Protection modes are designed to remain visible and bounded.

WHY IT’S USEFUL

Protection without surrendering control.

01

Reduce unnecessary AI cost

Local rules pre-filter traffic first; optional AI is reserved for cases that genuinely need a second opinion.

02

Make decisions explainable

Detection, classification and policy decisions are surfaced as evidence instead of disappearing inside opaque automation.

03

Keep mitigation controlled

Observation, challenge and defence modes let operators increase protection deliberately, with temporary and reversible actions.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES

What the product is optimised for

Privacy aware

Minimise unnecessary exposure of visitor data and keep local analysis as the default first step.

Bounded automation

Automated mitigations should have clear scope, duration and operator visibility.

Operational clarity

Show system health, detection history and current protection state in one operator-focused interface.

Progressive defence

Move from observation to challenge to stronger protection instead of treating every signal as a reason to block.
