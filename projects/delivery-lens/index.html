I use Scrum to create focus around outcomes and a regular inspect-and-adapt rhythm. I use Kanban practices to make the work visible, understand flow and respond transparently when priorities change.

THE PRINCIPLE

Use reporting to create transparency, not control.

I keep reporting focused on the Sprint Goal, meaningful changes and what the team learned. The purpose is to support inspection and adaptation, not to turn every work item into a management metric.

THE PRACTICE

Different practices solve different delivery problems.

Scrum gives the team focus and cadence. Kanban practices help make flow and blockers visible. CI/CD provides the engineering path from change to production. I use them together rather than forcing every part of delivery into one framework.

All Sprint figures, security patches and delivery scenarios shown here are synthetic. They demonstrate the approach without reproducing private programme information.
