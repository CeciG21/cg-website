AI Delivery Triage Assistant

A team-facing GPT assistant that helps identify candidate parent epics or features, find potentially related work, navigate boards and retrieve knowledge guidance. The examples use fictional work items.

Custom GPTAzure DevOps APIsKnowledge discoveryHuman-reviewed triage
THE PROBLEM

Delivery triage often depends on knowing where to look.

Work can be difficult to place when teams need to understand likely parent features, related items, board context and relevant delivery guidance across different sources.

THE APPROACH

Assist navigation without automating judgement.

The assistant searches connected delivery data and guidance, then returns candidate relationships and supporting context for a person to review. It is designed to reduce lookup effort, not make authoritative delivery decisions.

HOW IT HELPS

Find context faster.

Typical tasks include locating possible parent epics or features, identifying related work, finding the right board or backlog and retrieving relevant knowledge guidance.

DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Human-reviewed recommendations.

Suggestions remain explainable and reviewable. The assistant surfaces evidence and candidates while keeping ownership of triage and planning decisions with the delivery team.
